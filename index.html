Autonomy, from models to missions.

I am an aerospace engineer and doctoral researcher working in guidance, navigation and control (GNC). I connect estimation, simulation, and deployed robotics - from filter design and physics models to lab and field testing.

Currently a doctoral researcher at SnT, University of Luxembourg, working on Semantic Relational SLAM for UAVs with ROS 2 and PX4.

ROS 2 Jazzy and Gazebo Harmonic stack in C++17 with Eigen and OpenCV. I develop the odometry front ends, the error-state filter, Ackermann control and the rosbag plus Plotly review path. More information on scope and limits is on the projects page.

Simulation and estimation

Entry and Orbital Dynamics

Two compact codebases: an entry-vehicle trajectory simulator and a rigid-body and gravity framework with a CubeSat ADCS harness.

ArvSimulator - entry trajectory and atmosphere loop

A C90 and CMake physics core with a layered Earth atmosphere and six-degree-of-freedom trajectory integration. I implemented the atmosphere, RK4 and Dormand-Prince 5(4) solvers, and archive loop, with Python tooling to inspect the atmosphere model.

  • C90
  • CMake
  • RK4
  • Dormand-Prince 5(4)
  • Python plotting
More Info Source on GitHub

NBodySimulator - rigid-body gravity and ADCS harness

I developed NBodySimulator to simulate and test JamSail's ADCS: an Extended Kalman Filter fusing sun-sensor, magnetometer and IMU measurements, my own IGRF implementation, and a sun-sensing algorithm developed and tested in the simulator.

  • C90
  • CMake
  • EKF sensor fusion
  • IGRF
  • Embedded C
