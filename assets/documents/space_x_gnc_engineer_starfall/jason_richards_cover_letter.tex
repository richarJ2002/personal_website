        I am applying for the GNC Engineer (Starfall) position. My background combines
        a First-Class MEng in Aerospace Engineering, current doctoral research in

        sensors and hardware, analyze performance, document the design, and carry the
        system through testing. The opportunity to apply that approach to an entry
        capsule supporting logistics and in-space manufacturing is highly motivating.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        At Airbus Defence \& Space, I developed C/C++ software on Linux for an offline
        GNC simulation, coupling a natural-scene generator to produce synthetic imagery
        and LiDAR scans for algorithm development and evaluation. I also led LiDAR test
        activities during Integrated BreadBoarding 3 and 4: I designed a networked ROS
        sensor test bench, integrated it with rover breadboards, executed test
        procedures, and collected and analyzed campaign data. The campaigns supported
        an end-to-end Sample Fetch Rover concept-of-operations demonstration at TRL~6.
        This gave me practical experience connecting simulation models, GNC software,
        sensors, vehicle interfaces, performance data, and time-constrained
        operations in a multidisciplinary Agile team.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        As ADCS Lead for the JamSail 3U CubeSat, I translated the mission concept of
        operations into requirements, architecture, interfaces, and a verification
        plan. I developed embedded C flight software and a C/C++/CMake simulation of
        rigid-body dynamics, attitude estimation, and control across mission modes. The
        determination chain combined TRIAD with an EKF fusing sun-sensor,
        magnetometer, and IMU measurements, supported by sensor filtering and a C
        implementation of the IGRF environmental model. This work developed the state
        estimation, attitude-control, spacecraft-systems, and simulation-verification
        foundation central to Starfall GNC.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        I now develop and deploy C++ and Python autonomy software on physical UAVs using
        ROS~2, PX4, embedded Linux, camera/IMU sensing, and OpenVINS visual--inertial
        odometry. My work spans estimation, semantic mapping, planning, control,
        simulation, laboratory integration, flight testing, and performance analysis. I
        troubleshoot across software, sensing, estimation, and vehicle interfaces and
        build reproducible Docker, calibration, version-control, and technical
        documentation workflows. My research also includes shared semantic maps and
        mission-aware multi-vehicle coordination, directly complementing Starfall's
        interest in fleet-level logistics and recovery operations.%
    }

    \LetterFinalParagraph{%
