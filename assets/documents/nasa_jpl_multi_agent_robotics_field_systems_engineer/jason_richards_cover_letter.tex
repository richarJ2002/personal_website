% Section heading used to divide the letter into its four narrative parts.
% \addvspace prevents adjacent spacing commands from accidentally stacking.
\newcommand{\LetterSection}[1]{%
    \par\addvspace{\SectionBefore}%
    {\fontsize{\SectionSize}{\SectionLineSize}\selectfont
     \textbf{\textcolor{CVBlack}{#1}}\par}%
    \addvspace{\SectionAfter}%
}

% Standard body paragraph. Change only \ParagraphSpacing above to control the
% visible gap after every paragraph that uses this command.
\newcommand{\LetterParagraph}[1]{%
    \noindent #1\par
    \addvspace{\ParagraphSpacing}%
}

% Final body paragraph. It deliberately adds no paragraph gap because
% \ClosingBefore controls the distance to "Yours sincerely".
\newcommand{\LetterFinalParagraph}[1]{%
    \noindent #1\par
}

    \LetterParagraph{%
        Space exploration has been the defining focus of my career. I have followed
        JPL's Ingenuity mission closely since its arrival on Mars, and it became the
        mission that crystallised the direction I wanted my career to take. Ingenuity
        showed how an aerial robot could extend the reach of planetary exploration,
        which directly motivated my decision to pursue a PhD in aerial Semantic SLAM.
        That choice deliberately united my spacecraft and planetary-rover experience
        with deep, hands-on UAV robotics. The opportunity to help JPL field complex
        multi-agent aerospace systems for terrestrial and planetary exploration is
        therefore not simply aligned with my background; it is the goal towards which I
        have intentionally developed it.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        At Airbus Defence \& Space, I worked in the AOCS/GNC team on autonomous
        planetary-rover technology and led LiDAR test activities during Integrated
        BreadBoarding 3 and 4. These time-constrained campaigns supported an end-to-end
        Sample Fetch Rover concept demonstration at Technology Readiness Level 6. I
        designed a networked sensor test bench, integrated LiDAR with rover breadboards
        using ROS over a local wireless network, and collected and analysed campaign
        data. I also developed C/C++ software on Linux for an offline GNC simulation
        that generated synthetic imagery and LiDAR scans. This took my work from early
        feasibility through representative hardware integration and testing within a
        fast-paced, multidisciplinary team.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        I now develop and deploy autonomous UAV systems in the Autonomous Robotics
        Group (ARG), in Luxembourg, using ROS 2, PX4, embedded Linux, Raspberry
        Pi, cameras, IMUs, OpenVINS, Docker, and Kalibr. My work spans Semantic
        Relational SLAM, localisation, planning, control, simulation, laboratory
        integration, and flight testing, with the focus on integrating situational
        awareness into the autonomy pipeline. This includes collaborative missions based
        on shared semantic maps and multi-robot coordination. Before my PhD, I
        led the JamSail 3U CubeSat ADCS, developing embedded C software, an EKF
        for sun-sensor, magnetometer, and IMU fusion, and a C implementation of
        the IGRF magnetic-field model.%
    }

    \LetterParagraph{%
        These experiences match the central challenge of this role: moving research
        autonomy onto real, networked robotic vehicles and making the complete system
        work reliably under time pressure. I bring practical experience across
        ROS/ROS 2, C/C++, Python, embedded Linux, Docker, Raspberry Pi, PX4, sensor and
        payload integration, localisation, planning, control, communications, hardware,
        and testing. I also bring the systems judgement and soft skills needed to work
        across disciplines: translating mission needs into requirements and interfaces,
        communicating test status and integration issues clearly, and troubleshooting
        across software, sensors, networks, and hardware. My progression from UKSEDS

        national competitions with volunteers and industrial partners. This demonstrates
