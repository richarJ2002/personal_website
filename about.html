About

I am a robotics software developer and PhD researcher working at the intersection of robotic manipulation, motion planning, and human-robot interaction. My work focuses on developing algorithms and software systems that enable robots to operate reliably in unstructured, real-world environments.

I have experience across the robotics stack — from low-level control and perception to high-level task planning and system integration. My research has been published in top-tier robotics conferences and journals.

Beyond research, I contribute to open-source robotics software and am interested in making robotics more accessible through better tooling and education.
